Our faith

We Believe.

We believe in God the Father, in Jesus Christ the only begotten Son of God, and the Holy Spirit (Holy Ghost).

God loves the world.

We believe God loves the world. John 3:16–21

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life. For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved. He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God. And this is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil. For every one that doeth evil hateth the light, neither cometh to the light, lest his deeds should be reproved. But he that doeth truth cometh to the light, that his deeds may be manifest, that they are wrought in God.”

Faith in God.

We believe we must have faith in God (Mark 11:22); we must believe God (Romans 4:3, Galatians 3:6, James 2:23). Our righteousness is through faith in God through Jesus Christ.

Jesus Christ.

We believe Jesus, the Son of God and the Savior of the world, is the Way, the Truth, and the Life. John 14:6

The Holy Spirit.

We believe Jesus Christ left us the Holy Spirit of God to comfort us (John 14:16), to empower us (Acts 1:8, 2 Timothy 1:7), teach us (John 14:26, 1 John 2:27), guide us into all truth (John 16:13), show us things to come (John 16:13), and bring all things to our remembrance whatsoever Jesus said (John 14:26).

The Word of God.

We believe the Bible is the written Word of God, that “all scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, thoroughly furnished unto all good works.” 2 Timothy 3:16–17

We believe the people of God must study the Bible: “Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.” 2 Timothy 2:15

God is still speaking to the Body of Christ today through His Holy Spirit. We must make room in our lives for prayer, reading the Bible, and being still long enough to hear what the Holy Spirit is saying. Hearing and obeying the voice of the Holy Spirit is critical to pleasing God and living out our faith in Jesus Christ with victory.

The Gift of Salvation!

God loves you. He knows everything about you and still loves you. The Word of God tells us that all have sinned; and the payment for sin is death BUT the gift of God is eternal life. God sent His only begotten Son, Jesus Christ, to die for the sins of the world. Jesus Christ paid the price for sin, so anyone who believes in what Jesus did and follows Him will spend eternity in heaven! The Bible tells us that whosoever calls on the name of the Lord shall be saved.

The most important decision we will ever make is not who we will marry, what school we will attend, where we will work or live. The most important is whether we will choose to believe God, turn from sin (wrongdoing), and accept Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior.

Please ask Jesus to come into your life today, right now, by praying this simple prayer:

Lord Jesus, please save me. Please forgive me of my sins. I believe You died on the cross for my sins and rose from the dead. Please deliver me from my past and help me to obey God. I accept You as my Lord and Savior. Thank you for a new life. Thank you Father for hearing my prayer. In Jesus Christ name I pray. Amen.

If you prayed this prayer, you can be sure God heard you. You are saved! Do not focus on your past or being perfect; focus on knowing Jesus. The Holy Spirit will open your eyes and do the work on the inside of you. As you surrender your life to Jesus, day by day, choice by choice, you and others will see the difference.

If you prayed to be saved, please send us a message on the Let’s Connect page so we can celebrate with you!

We thank God for you, we are here to encourage you in your walk with Jesus Christ and will be praying for your continued spiritual growth.
