Our faith

We Believe.

We believe in God the Father, in Jesus Christ the Son of God, and the Holy Spirit (Holy Ghost).

God loves the world.

We believe God loves the world. John 3:16–21

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life. For God sent not his Son into the world to condemn the world; but that the world through him might be saved. He that believeth on him is not condemned: but he that believeth not is condemned already, because he hath not believed in the name of the only begotten Son of God. And this is the condemnation, that light is come into the world, and men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil. For every one that doeth evil hateth the light, neither cometh to the light, lest his deeds should be reproved. But he that doeth truth cometh to the light, that his deeds may be manifest, that they are wrought in God.”

Faith in God.

We believe we must have faith in God (Mark 11:22); we must believe God (Romans 4:3, Galatians 3:6, James 2:23). Our righteousness is through faith in God through Jesus Christ.

Jesus Christ.

We believe Jesus, the Son of God and the Savior of the world, is the Way, the Truth, and the Life. John 14:6

The Holy Spirit.

We believe Jesus left us the Holy Spirit of God to comfort us (John 14:16), to empower us (Acts 1:8, 2 Timothy 1:7), teach us (John 14:26, 1 John 2:27), guide us into all truth (John 16:13), show us things to come (John 16:13), and bring all things to our remembrance whatsoever Jesus said (John 14:26).

The Word of God.

We believe the Bible is the written Word of God, that “all scripture is given by inspiration of God, and is profitable for doctrine, for reproof, for correction, for instruction in righteousness: That the man of God may be perfect, thoroughly furnished unto all good works.” 2 Timothy 3:16–17

We believe the people of God must study the Bible: “Study to shew thyself approved unto God, a workman that needeth not to be ashamed, rightly dividing the word of truth.” 2 Timothy 2:15
